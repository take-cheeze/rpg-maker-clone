- **bc2cpp** names every producer of an unresolved receiver in its `POLY_DIAG` `origin=` label, so a receiver that joins several values reports `join:a.b` instead of only the arm written last in the instruction order. The label comes from BytecodeIR reaching definitions and falls back to the old walk when the IR refuses. Generated dispatch is unchanged. Covered by `scripts/bc2cpp/receiver_origin_join_check.rb`.
