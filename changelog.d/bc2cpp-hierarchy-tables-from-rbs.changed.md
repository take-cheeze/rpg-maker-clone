- **bc2cpp** derives its core class hierarchy used for type-fit checks (`IrTypeFeedback::CORE_PARENTS` / `CORE_MIXINS`) from the `core_hierarchy` section of `sig/bc2cpp/declarations.rbs` instead of a hand-written copy. `Module`, `Class`, `Exception`, `StandardError` and `Struct` now take part in those checks, and `Struct` includes `Enumerable`. The build fails if that section is missing or empty.
