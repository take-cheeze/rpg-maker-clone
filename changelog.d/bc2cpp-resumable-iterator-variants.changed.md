- The bc2cpp compiler now gives 14 more direct-yield core iterators a resumable variant beside their fast body, so a Fiber reaches them without a Ruby driver compiled at run time: `Array#collect!`, `delete_if`, `reject!`, `select!`, `Enumerable#sort`, `Hash#delete`, `Hash#each`, `Integer#downto`, `times`, `upto`, `String#each_char`, and `StringIO#each`, alongside the `Array#each` and `each_index` variants. A variant suspends at an explicit `block.call(...)` on the method's own block, as it does at `yield`; the other receivers keep the ordinary send. Methods whose block is called from an inner block (`Enumerable#collect`, `inject`, `max`, ...) stay on their drivers. `scripts/bc2cpp/resumable_variants_check.rb` runs each iterator both ways inside and outside a Fiber.
