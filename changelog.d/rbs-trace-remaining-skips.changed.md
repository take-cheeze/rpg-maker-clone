- **bc2cpp** `scripts/bc2cpp/rbs_suggestion_trace_check.rb` now also compares optional positional parameters (`?T`), the extra arguments of a rest parameter (`*T`, through its elements) and keyword parameters (`k: T` and `?k: T`) with the `opt`, `rest`, `kwreq` and `kwopt` rows of a `BC2CPP_TRACE_TYPES` dump. Block parameters, ivars, constants and `extend`-ed modules stay skipped: the dump does not link block rows to their method, records no ivar or constant rows, and the hierarchy rows record no `extend`. Documented in `docs/bc2cpp-ir-feedback.md`; each closed kind has a passing and a contradicting fixture in the gate self-test.
