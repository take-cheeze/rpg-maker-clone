- mruby (3rd/mruby) scales to a heap of gigabytes and hundreds of thousands of symbols, which bc2cpp needs to run as an mruby executable (docs/bc2cpp-self-compile.md): the symbol table's hash buckets grow with the symbol count (they were 256, so every lookup walked a chain proportional to the symbols); the gray-stack rescan resumes where it stopped (it restarted at the first page, a pass over the whole heap per 1024 gray objects); the incremental sweep keeps the free-page list instead of rebuilding it from every page after each step; `khash` no longer loops forever on a table whose empty buckets all became deleted ones (found as `Set#include?` spinning); `mrb_get_args` with 14 arguments and a keyword hash reads the block from the right slot (`Struct.new` of 14 members with `keyword_init:` raised "not a block"); and `Set` key comparison skips the protected funcall for identical objects, immediates and Strings. Measured on the same input, bc2cpp's analysis as an `-O2` mruby executable reaches its compile phase in 217 s (over 1,000 s before) and that phase finishes in 483 s per third of the owners (it never finished before). `MemberRecord#hash` covers the leading members, not every member (an Irep's hash walked its instructions and child ireps).
