- **bc2cpp** `scripts/bc2cpp/trace_merge.rb CANDIDATES.rbs` proposes the trace candidates that `sig/bc2cpp/declarations.rbs` does not already declare, written to `build/trace_proposals.rbs`. The file is outside `sig/`, so nothing loads it, and a method joins the declarations only after review. A candidate that conflicts with a declared signature is reported and left alone. `scripts/bc2cpp/trace_merge_check.rb` is the host-only check.
