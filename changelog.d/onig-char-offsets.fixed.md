- mruby-onig-regexp, with `MRB_UTF8_STRING`, reports `MatchData#begin`/`#end`/`#offset` in characters and takes the `pos` of `Regexp#match`/`match?` in characters, as CRuby does (they were bytes, so slicing a non-ASCII String by them was off, including the gem's own `String#[]=` and `=~`); a `/n` regexp keeps byte offsets. `Regexp.union` is added. Long substrings share the matched String's buffer, so `` $` `` and `$'` no longer copy the whole text per match. bc2cpp needs all three to run as an mruby executable.
