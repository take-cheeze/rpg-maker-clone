- **bc2cpp** `scripts/bc2cpp/rbs_suggestion_trace_check.rb` now compares an overloaded method (a method key with more than one candidate definition, or an RBS method with several overloads) when its definitions declare the same signature and the dump shows one implementation under the key. Disagreeing definitions, or a trace that shows two implementations under one key, are skipped and counted as `overloaded comparison(s) skipped` in the summary, never as a failure. The decidability rule is in `docs/bc2cpp-ir-feedback.md` (Overloaded methods); each case has a fixture in the gate self-test.
