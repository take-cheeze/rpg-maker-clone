- **Drain skills drain.** The skill row's `absorb_damage` (吸収) gives the caster
  the HP the skill takes; 13 of Nepheshel's skills and 5 of mtf-meido-action's
  set it and nothing read it, so every drain spell in both games was an ordinary
  attack spell. The rule that matters is the clamp: RPG_RT limits the effect to
  the target's current HP **before** applying it, so a 200-damage drain on a
  30 HP foe deals 30 and returns 30 — a drain is weaker against a nearly-dead
  target, not merely capped in what it gives back. The caster still stops at
  full. The battle log reports it with the 用語 pair that had nothing to report
  until now (「スライムのＨＰを 20 奪った！」 / 「リトはＨＰを 20 奪われた！」),
  which is close to the recovery line and differs in three places. Only offensive
  skills drain, as RPG_RT gates it. SP drain is left out: an RPG2000 skill has one
  flag rather than a pair and neither test bed has a negative-SP skill to measure
  it against. See ADR 0038.
