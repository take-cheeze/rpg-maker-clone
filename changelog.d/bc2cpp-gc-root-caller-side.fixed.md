- **bc2cpp** roots an element a compiled read takes out of an Array or Hash (`a[i]`, `h[k]`, and an inlined `each`, `map`, `inject` or `sort_by` over an Array) when its local is still live after a call that may unlink it from the container, such as `Array#clear`, `Hash#[]=` or a method called by name. A collection that frees such an element during the call no longer leaves the compiled caller reading a freed value. Covered by the new cases in `scripts/bc2cpp/gc_root_check.rb`.
