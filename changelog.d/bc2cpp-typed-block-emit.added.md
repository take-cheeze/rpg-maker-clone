- **Type checker** `--emit-rbs` now writes the classes a yielding method passes to its block as `{ (T1, ..., Tn) -> untyped }`, when its own yield sites prove them (a plain yielder with one argument count, no `&blk`, no `yield` in a nested block, no splatted or `call`-based yield). Positions it cannot prove stay `untyped`, and an unproven method keeps `{ (*untyped) -> untyped }`. Covered by `scripts/bc2cpp/block_yield_types_check.rb`.
