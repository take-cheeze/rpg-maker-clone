- **bc2cpp** checks the base section of `sig/bc2cpp/declarations.rbs` in CI. It fails when a committed native block differs from what `scripts/bc2cpp/base_rbs_all.rb` writes now, or when a Ruby declaration is missing or stale. The native blocks now map each gem to its tag explicitly (`BaseSection::TAGS`), and the committed base section is regenerated to match: `mruby-math-wio` has its own `base_math-wio` block, and its declarations are no longer repeated in `base_math`.
