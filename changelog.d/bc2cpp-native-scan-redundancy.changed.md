- The bc2cpp closed-world generator no longer repeats its native-source scans: `class_variable_map` is computed once per source, the `(\w+)\s*=` scan patterns skip mid-word starts, and `rom_entries` and the `boot_defclass` scan skip sources that cannot match. A one-method call takes 3.5 s instead of 5.6 s and `numeric_slow` about 400 s instead of 576 s. The generated C++ is unchanged.
