- **bc2cpp** `BC2CPP_TRACE_TYPES=1` now also records the receiver of a singleton method (`def self.x`, `def Owner.x`, the defs of a `class << self`) as a `self` PARAM_SITE row, keyed by the class object it ran on rather than its metaclass. `scripts/bc2cpp/rbs_suggestion_trace_check.rb` compares it with `Owner` through `--hierarchy`: the class itself or a reachable subclass is admitted, a complete chain that misses it is a contradiction, and anything it cannot place (a module owner, a non-class receiver, no hierarchy, an overflowed site) is counted and skipped. The dump format is unchanged for every other row, so older dumps and reports read as before. Checked by `scripts/bc2cpp/param_trace_return_check.rb` and the gate self-test.
