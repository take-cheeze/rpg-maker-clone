- **bc2cpp** reads more of its analysis from the bytecode IR. Struct and singleton receivers (`k = Struct; k.new`, `def o.m` on a local) are recognized through a dominating walk, so a Struct held in a local now registers its members and block defs, and a receiver that is a Struct only on one arm of a join is no longer taken for one. The nested-block upvar walks share one walker. Generated code is unchanged. Covered by `scripts/bc2cpp/struct_receiver_check.rb` and `scripts/bc2cpp/upvar_walks_check.rb`.
